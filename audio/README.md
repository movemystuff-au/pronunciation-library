These recordings play at the speaker’s original pace with Listen and at 70% with Slow. In the local macOS preview, unrecorded entries use the installed Australian voice to produce one clip for both speeds. The static site uses browser speech synthesis for those entries. The browser and device determine the available voice quality and whether the requested rate is honoured.
